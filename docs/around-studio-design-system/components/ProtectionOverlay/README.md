Shows the areas the engine keeps clear for real controls, and why an artwork is limited.

- Draw each protected envelope with `around-protect`: the 45° vermilion hatch (the one diagonal in the system) over `vermilion-wash`, a ruled vermilion edge, and a mono label naming the part (`player.controls.bottom`).
- Show it when the creator turns on 保護範囲を表示, and automatically around the area that just limited a layer.
- Pair it with a 制限中 chip on the layer row and a `around-notice` that states what was protected and how to fix it.

Do not: draw the overlay outside editing; hide it behind author layers; use the hatch or any 45° shape for anything else.
